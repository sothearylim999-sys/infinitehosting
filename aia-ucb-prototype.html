Partnership prototype · Credit card payment & installment

AIA × UCB Credit Card — Premium Payment & Installment Journey

Customers pay an AIA premium and may choose to pay directly by UCB Credit Card or apply for a UCB installment plan. The existing AIA payment gateway still processes the money. UCB adds the installment selection, calculator, request capture, and post-payment setup — captured through a UCB-controlled channel so AIA does not need to forward customer data manually.
